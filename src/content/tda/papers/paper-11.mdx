## Overview

The paper treats a working life as a trajectory through a space of employment states,
observed over up to three decades of UK panel data, and asks what *shape* the
collection of such trajectories has. The substantive claim is that career inequality
is not adequately described as position on a one-dimensional mobility scale. It is
better described as location within a structured geometry of regimes, separated by
boundaries that are rarely crossed.
