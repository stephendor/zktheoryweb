## Overview

The paper addresses a gap between practice and inference: persistent homology is now
routinely computed from social data, but the question of *when a topological feature is
statistically significant* has frequently been answered informally. The contribution is
a set of explicit null models and test statistics that make the question answerable with
calibrated error control, adapted to the sampling structure of longitudinal panels.
