# Reporting and data analysis for small organisations

<p class="lead">Reliable reports, and the analysis that makes them useful — for charities, training providers and alternative provision in Dorset and across the UK.</p>

Most small organisations produce the same figures several times over: for funders, for trustees, for commissioners, for inspection. Each time, someone rebuilds a spreadsheet, chases missing numbers and hopes the totals agree. I turn that into one checked, repeatable process that runs on the systems you already use — and then use the data to show you something you didn't know.

## Your data

Most work needs no personal data at all. Each piece of work sits at one of three levels, and you know which before it starts.

- **Summary figures (the default).** Totals, counts and anonymised tables, with no names or personal details. Counts below a set threshold are combined or left out, so no figure points to one person. The Review always works this way, as do most focused analyses and most days of support. For the need and reach analysis and the outcome measure analysis, the raw data stays on your own computer and I receive only counts or summary tables.
- **Pseudonymised records.** Rows with codes instead of names: the self-assessment data pack and the commissioner outcome report. That is still personal data, so this runs only under a written data processing agreement, and the data is deleted when the work ends.
- **Working in your systems.** Cleaning up records means seeing them, so I work inside your own CRM or shared drive with an account you control, rather than taking copies away. This also runs under a written agreement, and my access is removed when the work ends.

If work under a bank of days needs pseudonymised records, we sign a data processing agreement before that work starts.

I use the tools you already have — Excel, Google Sheets, your MIS or CRM exports — rather than asking you to buy new software.

## Common questions

<div class="service-faq">

### Do we need to change our systems?

No. I work with what you already use and make it reliable.

### How much of our time does it take?

For the Review, a 60–90 minute conversation and some summary figures. For a Build, a few short check-ins and one training session.

### What if our data is messy?

That's normal and it's usually the point. The checks I build show you exactly where the gaps are, so they get fixed at the source.

### Is this monitoring and evaluation?

It's the data side of it. Monitoring and evaluation training helps you decide what to measure and how to present it. I do the next step: getting reliable figures out of the records you already keep, checking them, and turning them into the reports funders and trustees ask for.

### How is this different from an evaluation report written from an interview?

Those are written from a conversation about the project. I work from your records, so every figure can be traced back and checked, and you keep the workbook to reuse next time.

### Do you charge VAT?

No. I'm not VAT-registered, so no VAT is added to any price. That matters for most small charities, which can't reclaim VAT.

### Can we fund this from a grant?

Many charities include this kind of work in applications for organisational development or core costs. I can give you a costed paragraph for your application. Check your funder's criteria first. If you're asking a funder for core costs, the full cost analysis gives you the figures to justify the request.

### What's in the free summary, and what does it cost?

It's free. It's one page built from public data only: the Charity Commission register and 360Giving for charities, the Department for Education's published achievement rates for training providers, or the published figures for your council areas for alternative provision. I don't need any of your internal data. I send it by email, and you choose whether to reply.

### Is the deprivation check really free?

Yes. It's a spreadsheet you keep, and it gives you the headline: how many of the people you work with live in England's most deprived neighbourhoods. The paid [need and reach analysis](/services/sheets/need-and-reach-analysis/) adds the detail.

### Can you help with the impact section of our trustees' report?

Yes. The [impact evidence pack](/services/sheets/impact-evidence-pack/) gives you outputs, outcomes, reach and volunteer hours, checked against your records, in a form you can lift into the report.

### How do we pay?

The Review is invoiced when you book and paid before the report is delivered. Builds are 50% at the start and 50% on handover. Focused analyses are invoiced when you book and paid on delivery. Single days are invoiced when the work is done, banks of days are invoiced in full at the start, and the Briefing is invoiced monthly, or quarterly for the quarterly-only option.
