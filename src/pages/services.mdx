# Reporting and data analysis for small organisations

<p class="lead">Reliable reports, and the analysis that makes them useful — for charities, training providers and alternative provision in Dorset and across the UK.</p>

Most small organisations produce the same figures several times over: for funders, for trustees, for commissioners, for inspection. Each time, someone rebuilds a spreadsheet, chases missing numbers and hopes the totals agree. I turn that into one checked, repeatable process that runs on the systems you already use — and then use the data to show you something you didn't know.

## What you get

<section class="service-segment" id="charities" aria-labelledby="charities-heading">

<h3 id="charities-heading">Charities and community groups</h3>

- **One pack for funders and trustees**, built from your existing spreadsheets, with figures that add up back to your activity records.
- **One set of definitions** — what counts as a participant, a session, an outcome — used for every funder, so your numbers stop disagreeing.
- **Evidence for your next bid or renewal**: trends by project, site and month; reach against target; cost per participant or outcome; volunteer hours and their equivalent value.

</section>

<section class="service-segment" id="training-providers" aria-labelledby="training-providers-heading">

<h3 id="training-providers-heading">Apprenticeship and training providers</h3>

- **A pre-submission check every month**: learners whose planned end dates, breaks in learning or off-the-job hours don't match across your systems, listed before the ILR goes in.
- **Funding forecast against actuals**: expected and actual earnings by month and standard, with learners drifting past planned end dates flagged early.
- **Retention and achievement analysis**: where withdrawals concentrate by standard, employer, cohort and caseload, compared with national rates — ready for your self-assessment.

</section>

<section class="service-segment" id="alternative-provision" aria-labelledby="alternative-provision-heading">

<h3 id="alternative-provision-heading">Alternative provision and tuition providers</h3>

- **A ready-made summary for each commissioning school or local authority**: attendance, sessions delivered and progress, built from your existing registers.
- **Early flags**: placements where attendance has dropped for three weeks or more, and missing registers, spotted before a review rather than during one.
- **Analysis for commissioning conversations**: which referral routes and placement patterns go with sustained attendance and successful moves on, and hours delivered against hours commissioned and invoiced.

</section>

## Your data

I work from summary figures and anonymised records. I don't need names or personal details to start, and I never need them for the Review. If a Build does need record-level data, we agree exactly what, how it's handled and when it's deleted, in writing, before anything is shared.

I use the tools you already have — Excel, Google Sheets, your MIS or CRM exports — rather than asking you to buy new software.

## About

I'm Stephen Dorman, based in Dorset. I combine a mathematics and statistics background with years of hands-on work in education and in managing multi-site teams, including specialist SEND support in schools and coordinating volunteers for a national charity. I know what it is to produce figures under deadline and to be on the receiving end of them.

My research on this site looks at how statistics has shaped the way poverty is measured. The same principle runs through this work: numbers that decide things about people should be counted carefully and explained plainly.

## Common questions

### Do we need to change our systems?

No. I work with what you already use and make it reliable.

### How much of our time does it take?

For the Review, a 60–90 minute conversation and some summary figures. For a Build, a few short check-ins and one training session.

That's normal and it's usually the point. The checks I build show you exactly where the gaps are, so they get fixed at the source.

### Can we fund this from a grant?

Many charities include this kind of work in applications for organisational development or core costs. I can give you a costed paragraph for your application. Check your funder's criteria first.

The Review is invoiced when you book and paid before the report is delivered. Builds are 50% at the start and 50% on handover.
